Privacy Policy — Display Control

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Display Control ("the App", "we", "us") is a macOS menu bar utility for controlling external display hardware (brightness, contrast, color, volume) and audio output.

TL;DR

The App runs entirely on your Mac. It does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no network service.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone, location)
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reports (unless you explicitly choose to share one with us by email)
  • No advertising identifiers or device fingerprinting

Data Stored Locally

The App stores only your preferences on your own Mac (via the standard macOS UserDefaults), for example:

  • Brightness / contrast / RGB / volume settings
  • Your brightness-calibration curve
  • Dual-screen feature toggles and saved state

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Permissions

The App may request the following macOS permissions, used only for their stated purpose:

  • Accessibility — to intercept media-key (volume) presses so you can control volume from the keyboard.
  • Audio / Core Audio — to create and manage an aggregate audio device for simultaneous playback on two devices.

No permission is used to read, collect, or transmit your data.

Third-Party Components

The App bundles m1ddc, an open-source command-line tool (MIT License) used to send DDC/CI commands to your display over the local connection. m1ddc runs locally and does not communicate with any network service.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent any information, because we do not collect any information.
